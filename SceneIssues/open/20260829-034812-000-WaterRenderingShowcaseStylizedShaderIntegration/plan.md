## Cost / blast radius
Six 32-entry `Vector4` water tables cost 3,072 bytes plus one uint semantic water mask. Explicit arena addressing adds one scalar integer to the existing per-water-draw property block and no draw call/allocation. Lip/base/edge/spray semantics reuse the already-reserved high byte of `SmoothSurfaceVertex.Material`, so vertex stride remains 32 bytes and there is still one canonical water buffer/draw path. Impact spray adds one ordinary quad only at an exposed vertical lower boundary; non-waterfall profiles clip it in the shader. Final exact-player telemetry and arena geometry counts must prove that incremental geometry remains within budget; unavailable FrameTimingManager GPU values must not be invented.
