Every target still waits on real content settlement plus renderer coverage; no synthetic completion or pre-marking is allowed.
- Move generic-settlement framing directly into `SettlementSurvey`: camera X/Z remains near the computed settlement bounding-box focus with only a small horizontal offset, and camera elevation is high enough for a steep overview that contains the four already-validated building plots while motor/streaming demand remains grounded at semantic focus.

- Remove `KentridgeMacroWorldSettlementEvidenceCamera.cs` and its `.meta` after the driver owns these responsibilities; this avoids two competing evidence-camera/scheduler paths and reduces blast radius.
- Keep Fairy, Orc, Southern Ridge/pass, and network targets intact; all still require real readiness + renderer coverage before capture.
