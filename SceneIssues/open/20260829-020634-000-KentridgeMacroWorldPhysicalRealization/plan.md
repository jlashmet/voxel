## Selected fix under validation
The validated 102.4 m Kentridge completeness radius and survey-demand alignment remain retained. The current executable gate is the generic renderer count-batch fairness correction:

1. `TryDispatchCountBatch` only appends immutable descriptors; a freshly filled low-index lane cannot immediately bypass older queued lanes.
2. `AdvanceCountBatches` starts from `s_CountBatchSealCursor`, considers full lanes immediately and partial lanes after the existing bounded fill delay, and submits at most one lane because the existing global Metal dispatch token remains authoritative.
3. `SealCountBatch` reports whether a GPU submission actually occurred; fence/token backpressure returns false without rotating priority.
4. After successful submission the cursor advances to the following lane, so sustained refill produces `0,1,2,3,...` service rather than `0,0,0,...`.
5. `ResetCountBatches` resets the fairness cursor with the rest of the world-scoped batching state.
6. `GpuCountBatchLaneFairnessTests.SaturatedCountBatchLanesUseRoundRobinSealAuthorityWithoutInlineBypass` protects the shared renderer behavior independently of Kentridge scene coordinates.

Rejected alternatives remain: do not widen Kentridge load radius, weaken `HasCompletePublishedNearSurfaceCoverage`, increase extraction concurrency/device budgets, change renderer coverage semantics, hardcode settlement-specific rendering policy, or rerun a known acceptance-red source.

Experiments 030-039 retain prior CI/cost/equivalence/acceptance evidence. `experiment-040-gpu-count-batch-lane-starvation.md` is the current two-fix root-cause isolate and selected-fix rationale.
