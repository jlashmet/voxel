# R08 — Execution checklist

Budget: 45–60 minutes active work, excluding queued CI. Follow [plan.md](plan.md).

- [ ] Identify one edit with a screen-visible silhouette/opening effect and map its authoritative identity/revision and near/far owners.
- [ ] Capture before/edit/far/return views with source revisions and actual residency; never change interest radius or world truth to simulate distance.
- [ ] Determine whether semantic proxy, arbitrary-voxel fallback and terrain overlap, omit or resurrect geometry; distinguish delayed readiness from unsupported representation.
- [ ] Deliver the exact failing handoff and minimum edited-far-state requirement, or evidence it already works. No persistence or far-mesh redesign.
- [ ] Record findings, artifact provenance and limitations; update the concise plan with material results and falsified hypotheses. A test failure may be the investigation result, never a successful validation claim.
- [ ] Review the issue-only diff and complete documentation checks. Close only if investigation acceptance is satisfied; otherwise retain the blocker in `open/`. Follow the canonical PR/merge workflow and verify the result on master.
