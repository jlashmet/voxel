# Final concept visual review

Required bar: implementation-guiding **production concept art**, not a primitive/count blockout. Review is of the final committed town packs only; earlier rejected schematic studies are excluded.

| Town | Classification | Visual relationship that passes |
|---|---|---|
| Kentridge | production-quality concept reference | Low organic foothill massing, warm plaster/timber over stone, modest civic scale, working frontage and well/supply square form one coherent practical town rather than a castle-city recolor. |
| Hightown | production-quality concept reference | Pale-stone/slate church silhouette remains dominant across the stepped terrace language; stairs, retaining levels and under-church/interior structure make elevation part of architecture rather than background decoration. |
| Moordell | production-quality concept reference | Formal limestone/burgundy symmetry, broad civic frontage and fountain/distribution space visibly separate privileged planning from Kentridge/Hightown's constrained settlement patterns. |
| Rossdam | production-quality concept reference | Red masonry, dark timber, fortified gate/command mass and dense equipment-shop frontage produce a guarded market axis with believable wall/road/room relationships. |
| Orc Village / Orc-Town | production-quality concept reference | Heavy timber/basalt construction, broad longhall/workshop roofs, forge heat and central feast/work court communicate craft and community without ramshackle or caricatured treatment. |
| Fairy Village | production-quality concept reference | Tree-integrated curved platforms, bridges, root-level circulation and luminous crystal/glass accents establish vertical woodland construction; interiors continue the timber/crystal language rather than becoming generic tavern rooms. |
| Cambridge | production-quality concept reference | Water is structural: canal/harbor blocks, stone quays, multiple bridges, docks/piles, slipway/crane, water-facing rooms, water stairs, tide/cistern infrastructure and reflected public space make the town read as water-centric before color. |

## Cross-town anti-drift review

The seven concepts do not rely on a single recolored house kit. Their grayscale/topology signatures remain distinct: Kentridge = organic well-centered lanes; Hightown = steep church terraces; Moordell = formal plaza/avenue; Rossdam = gate/market axis; Orc Village = heavy longhall/forge court; Fairy Village = tree platforms/bridges; Cambridge = canals/quays/bridge arches.

Common Project North Star continuity is intentionally limited to authored proportions, readable shapes, natural material families, warm/cool lighting logic, tactile construction cues and environment-first framing. Town-specific roofline, terrain, infrastructure, palette and economic/cultural reads remain separate.
