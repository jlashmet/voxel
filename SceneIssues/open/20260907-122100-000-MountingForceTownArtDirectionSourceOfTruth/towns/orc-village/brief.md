# Orc Village / Orc-Town concept brief

Source inventory is locked in `../../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md` before concept generation.

**Identity:** confident clan-built community organized around craft, shared meals, protection and hospitality; robust, never caricatured as crude or disposable. **Architecture:** massive timber frames, dark basalt/rubble plinths, broad low roofs, smoke vents, carved posts and woven awnings. **Palette:** charcoal basalt, smoke-browned timber, forged iron, ochre clay, moss green and rust-red textiles. **Terrain/infrastructure:** exposed rock, hard-packed roads, palisade/stone gate, drainage channels, forge yards, communal feast court, loading racks and well/cistern. **Source-backed anchors:** main village, armor/weapon/magic shops, pub, feast event and Orc-Town battle/cutscene material. **Six-image read:** craft-settlement overall; armor/weapon workshop exterior; pub/longhall/magic exterior; forge/workshop interior; feast/pub/shop interior; workshop/feast public court.

The six role views consume `concept-atlas.svg`; environment reference only, with no character or UI design.
