# Moordell concept brief

Source inventory is locked in `../../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md` before concept generation.

**Identity:** privileged imperial-aligned noble town where public abundance and patronage are visually unmistakable. **Architecture:** creamy limestone, dark timber, steep burgundy slate/tile, tall windows, carved lintels, ironwork and formal courtyards. **Palette:** cream, burgundy, imperial red, dark timber, brass/gold and restrained evergreen. **Terrain/infrastructure:** broad planned avenues, formal drains, fountain/distribution plaza, walled gardens, iron gates and carriage courts. **Source-backed anchors:** distribution setting/event, mayor interaction, armor/magic/weapon shops, inn, pub, grave and Gertrude house/underground route. **Six-image read:** planned noble town; civic/distribution exterior; noble/commercial exterior; civic/distribution interior; noble/commercial interior; fountain/distribution public space.

The six role views consume `concept-atlas.svg`; environment reference only, with no character or UI design.
