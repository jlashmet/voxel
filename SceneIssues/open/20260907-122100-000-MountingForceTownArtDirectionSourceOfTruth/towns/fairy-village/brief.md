# Fairy Village concept brief

Source inventory is locked in `../../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md` before concept generation.

**Identity:** airy woodland settlement woven through ancient trunks, roots, flowers, and luminous mineral/glass details; enchanting while still physically buildable. **Architecture:** curved laminated timber, root-hugging platforms, leaf-like roof shells, slender bridges, round/arched windows and occasional translucent crystal/glass panels. **Palette:** pale warm wood, moss/fern green, turquoise/teal, lilac/flower accents, milky crystal and warm gold practical lights. **Terrain/infrastructure:** multi-level giant-tree grove, root terraces, cave edge, elevated bridges/walkways, root stairs, rainwater channels, lantern paths and a market clearing. **Source-backed anchors:** main village, treehouse, inn, pub, accessory/magic/weapon shops, Mary and Rita houses, village cave and original background reference art. **Six-image read:** multi-level tree-village overall; treehouse/residence exterior; shop/inn exterior; residence/treehouse interior; shop/inn/pub interior; bridge-linked root/market public space.

The six role views consume `concept-atlas.svg`; environment reference only, with no character or UI design.
