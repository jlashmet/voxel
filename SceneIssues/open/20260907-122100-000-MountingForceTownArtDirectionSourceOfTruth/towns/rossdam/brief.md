# Rossdam concept brief

Source inventory is locked in `../../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md` before concept generation.

**Identity:** defensible royal-market town where command authority and equipment trade overlap. **Architecture:** iron-rich red stone/brick, soot-dark half timber, iron-braced gates, compact towered civic compound and covered shop rows. **Palette:** rust red, charcoal, cream plaster and oxidized copper/teal accents. **Terrain/infrastructure:** compact wall/gate loop, paved market road, covered frontage, guard stairs, service alleys and cart courts. **Source-backed anchors:** king's chamber, Rorik house, armor/magic/weapon shops, Henry quest material and Rossdam battle/arrest content. **Six-image read:** fortified market/command overall; royal-command exterior; equipment-trade exterior; king/command interior; equipment-shop interior; guarded market street/gate approach.

The six role views consume `concept-atlas.svg`; environment reference only, with no character or UI design.
