It samples the entire long route at an effectively fixed 13-voxel interval and carves one vertically aligned cylinder per sample with a flat top/bottom. Radius and lateral jitter vary, but the repeated vertical-axis topology naturally creates the wall scallops/ribs and ceiling bands visible throughout the exact built-player capture. `UndergroundCavernCirculationProtection` uses a similar topology over only the short destination span and therefore cannot explain the entire descent.

3. Replace the strict periodic one-cylinder topology with deterministic variable-distance primary nodes plus overlapping side/upper lobes. Preserve a guaranteed floor-level walkable core at every node; use offset upper/side lobes to break vertical wall/ceiling repetition without weakening normal movement or creating showcase coordinates.
4. Expose a deterministic naturalization plan/invariants that focused tests can inspect: bounded spacing, guaranteed overlap/core clearance, multiple spacing/side/ceiling variants, stable repeated resolution, and bounded lobe count.
