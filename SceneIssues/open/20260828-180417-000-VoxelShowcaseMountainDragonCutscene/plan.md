## Root-cause isolation after third failed attempt
`experiment-010-switchback-core-gap-minimal-repro.md` isolates the original geometry mismatch without another production edit. The original fixed-Z tiers began outside the tapered core while retaining a constant 360-voxel upper run that could not fit the near-summit radius. Cosmetic support-pair changes could not make that topology read as one coherent mountain without retaining large freestanding supports.

The branch now contains a first tapered-route implementation that fixes one half of that cause: `MountainLandmarkSpec.GetTierRouteGeometry` narrows X run deterministically with elevation and both authoring and route evidence consume that helper. Inspection against the stricter acceptance exposed a remaining correctness defect rather than a new aesthetic preference: each 46-voxel rise moves the tapered core near face inward by about 69 voxels in Z, while the walking lane remains at one fixed Z and is only 30 voxels wide. The lane overlaps the shell at the tier's low end but its high edge ends roughly 49 voxels outside the core at the tier's high end. The current focused regression permits that gap, so the shell-hugging task is not complete.

## Selected next fix
Keep the tapered-run helper and correct the remaining shell relationship semantically. Before editing production geometry, inspect the reusable ramp/shape primitive semantics to determine whether one rising segment can follow both the X route and receding Z shell, or whether a logical tier must be represented by a small deterministic sequence of shell-following subsegments. Whichever representation is chosen must be exposed by one shared route-geometry API and consumed by support authoring, headroom carving, path surface emission, and route/evidence waypoints so geometry and traversal cannot drift.

The path must hug the mountain shell rather than move deep into the core: at both ends of every rising segment/tier, the lane must overlap the natural flank enough to read as an exposed cut terrace. It must not remain wholly outside the core (the rejected freestanding causeway/berm look), and it must not sit wholly inside the core where the configured headroom carve would create a trench/tunnel. Lower tiers may remain broad and upper tiers must continue narrowing deterministically with elevation. Preserve normal motor traversal, turn landings, configured headroom/support, winding direction changes, summit approach and placeholder support. Keep residual embankment modest and support-raster cost flat or lower.

Do not broaden the summit core merely to preserve the old constant upper run, widen the road to conceal the shell gap, multiply visual support primitives speculatively, or perform another cosmetic support-pair iteration.
