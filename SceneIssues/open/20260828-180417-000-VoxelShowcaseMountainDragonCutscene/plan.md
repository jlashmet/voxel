The next discriminator is the unchanged curved `RasteriseBoundaryHalo` pass. It still calls `TryBoundaryDistanceQ4` (including integer square-root work for frusta) for every voxel in every expanded frustum bounds block, then discards deep-interior samples with `abs(distanceQ4) > 32`. For a convex frustum, a complete 8^3 block whose eight extreme voxel centres all have signed interior distance >32 Q4 cannot contain a boundary-halo sample: radial clearance is minimized at a rectangular radial-plane corner, axial cap clearance is minimized at an axial endpoint, and frustum radius varies monotonically between the two axial endpoints. Therefore only those proven deep-interior `Fill + Frustum` blocks may skip the halo voxel loop; all near-surface/partial blocks must retain the exact existing path.

Before production implementation, commit `b17d748f149b...` strengthened the focused regression to require both sides of the authored frustum boundary halo (positive solid-side and negative empty-side samples) while also proving a deep-interior cell has no authored boundary metadata.
