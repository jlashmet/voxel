Exact request `5cc2ef4e03ce...` (parent/source `bf376936ae66...`), run `33288586966`, proved that change insufficient: cache miss, ~63.1 s cold asset import, then the fresh bake was killed at 241 s / 10.1 GB before the focused test. The next discriminator was the unchanged curved `RasteriseBoundaryHalo` pass. It called `TryBoundaryDistanceQ4` (including integer square-root work for frusta) for every voxel in every expanded frustum bounds block, then discarded deep-interior samples with `abs(distanceQ4) > 32`. For a convex frustum, a complete 8^3 block whose eight extreme voxel centres all have signed interior distance >32 Q4 cannot contain a boundary-halo sample: radial clearance is minimized at a rectangular radial-plane corner, axial cap clearance is minimized at an axial endpoint, and frustum radius varies monotonically between the two axial endpoints.

Before production implementation, commit `b17d748f149b...` strengthened the focused regression to require both sides of the authored frustum boundary halo (positive solid-side and negative empty-side samples) while also proving a deep-interior cell has no authored boundary metadata. Production commit `da45c64e9658...` now skips only complete `Fill + Frustum` halo blocks whose eight extreme centres all exceed +32 Q4. It replaces up to 512 signed-distance/integer-sqrt evaluations with eight corner evaluations; near-surface, partial, outside, carve, paint, and non-frustum blocks retain the exact previous per-voxel halo path.
