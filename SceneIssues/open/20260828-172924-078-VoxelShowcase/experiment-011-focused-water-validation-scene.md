# Experiment 011 — focused water validation scene

## Hypothesis
The final gate was timing out because it rebuilt/baked the entire `VoxelShowcase` scene even though the regression only needs to prove that startup far terrain preserves authored water. A tiny scene can exercise the same production `VoxelFarTerrain` startup path and provide stable built-player screenshots within the five-minute CI envelope.

## Action
Added `Assets/Scenes/WaterStartupFallbackValidation.unity` with a validation-only runtime bootstrap. It seeds the same known 51.2 m authored-water region used by `StartupFallbackPreservesAuthoredWaterHeightAndMaterial`, creates production `VoxelFarTerrain` with a small 30–180 m clipmap, waits through its first `LateUpdate`, then freezes independent copies of the production-generated synchronous/fallback meshes. The camera looks directly into that authored-water region. `tools/showcase-player-capture.sh` maps only the exact regression filter to this scene for a 20 s real-player review.

## Why freeze
The emergency fallback is intentionally transient; in a tiny unloaded scene the authoritative rings can publish before CI's first 10 s screenshot. Freezing the first-frame meshes is validation presentation only: the mesh geometry, vertex water albedo, and authored lowered height are generated by production `VoxelFarTerrain` before copying. Normal scenes never instantiate this bootstrap.

## Blast radius / cost
No production rendering behavior changed. One validation scene/component and one exact-filter capture mapping were added. The validation scene allocates a two-region in-memory store, 256 authored water samples, and a 30–180 m far clipmap only while that scene is running. No full showcase world generation or castle bake is required.

## Verdict / next
Run exact-SHA targeted PlayMode CI with the existing behavioral regression, omit `scene_issue`, and request a 20 s replay. Promotion requires a green request plus inspection of the focused built-player screenshots showing the authored water surface.
