## Frozen canonical roster

The assignment covers exactly seven settlements:

1. Kentridge
2. Hightown
3. Moordell
4. Rossdam
5. Orc Village / Orc-Town
6. Fairy Village
7. Cambridge

Wharfington is **not** in the canonical roster: it was an unverified lead with no corroborating match in the pinned original project manifest or secondary repository. Forest, Graveyard, Logan-Castle, caves, battle areas, junctions, and other encounter/travel locations are likewise not towns for this pack.

## Shared Project North Star language

- Storybook stylization with broad readable silhouettes, slightly exaggerated rooflines/doorways/supports, tactile material breakup, and restrained hand-crafted irregularity.
- Modern 3D interpretation of historical identity rather than one-to-one reproduction of the original 2D rendering or layout.
- Exterior structure, terrain integration, infrastructure, and interiors must agree physically and materially.
- Town identity must survive grayscale: topology, silhouette, materials, infrastructure, economy/culture, and landmark hierarchy do more work than palette alone.
- Existing voxel-town imagery is implementation history, not art-direction authority.

## Anti-drift rules

1. Never substitute Wharfington for Orc Village or Fairy Village; the seven-town roster above is frozen.
2. Never recolor one generic medieval kit seven ways.
3. Cambridge owns the strong canal/dock water-town identity; water must be structural, not decorative.
4. Orc Village must read robust, crafted, communal, and inhabited rather than crude or disposable.
5. Fairy Village must remain physically buildable despite its enchanted tree/root language.
6. Moordell must read richer and more ordered than Kentridge/Hightown; Hightown must read steeper and more austere than Kentridge.
7. Rossdam must read more fortified/royal-market and equipment-trade focused than Kentridge's mixed civic commerce.
8. Every concept maps to the locked inventory in the primary source and the co-located town brief.
