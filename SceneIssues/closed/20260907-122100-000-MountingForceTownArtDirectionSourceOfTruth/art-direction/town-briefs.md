# Canonical Town Brief and Inventory Index

This is a convenience index to the locked pre-concept inventories. Detailed source-backed versus derived facts live in [`../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md`](../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md) and the seven co-located [`../towns/`](../towns/) briefs. Those files were established before concept production and remain the authoritative detail; this index deliberately avoids maintaining a second divergent canon.

## Roster lock

Exactly seven settlements are canonical: **Kentridge, Hightown, Moordell, Rossdam, Orc Village / Orc-Town, Fairy Village, and Cambridge**. Wharfington is excluded because no corroborating asset-path/source evidence was found in the pinned original or secondary repositories.

## Kentridge
- Source-backed anchors: Mayor House, Armor Shop, Magic Shop, Church, Awon House, Rebecca House, Well, Inn, Abandoned House, Sarah House, Katie House, Warehouse/lower level.
- Identity: welcoming mixed-trade civic/market town under food/supply pressure.
- Concept roles: well/market-centered overall; Magic Shop exterior; Church exterior; Inn interior; Magic Shop interior; well/market public space.
- Detail: [`../towns/kentridge/brief.md`](../towns/kentridge/brief.md).

## Hightown
- Source-backed anchors: Mayor House group, Armor Shop, Magic Shop, Weapon Shop, Timmy House, Church/under-church complex, Hightown Cave, distribution scene/site.
- Identity: steep upland working town with ration/distribution pressure and strong church/community presence.
- Concept roles: terraced overall; Weapon Shop exterior; Church exterior; Magic Shop interior; Timmy House interior; ration/distribution square.
- Detail: [`../towns/hightown/brief.md`](../towns/hightown/brief.md).

## Moordell
- Source-backed anchors: Moordell Building 1, mayor/civic interaction, distribution scene/site, Gertrude House in the underground, Moordell Underground, Wizard Trials reference, grave/battle reference.
- Identity: prosperous formal town of lords/nobles with privileged civic/supply hierarchy.
- Concept roles: ordered noble-town overall; noble townhouse exterior; Mayor/Distribution Hall exterior; noble receiving room; civic distribution hall; formal supply/fountain plaza.
- Detail: [`../towns/moordell/brief.md`](../towns/moordell/brief.md).

## Rossdam
- Source-backed anchors: King's chamber, Rorik house, Armor Shop, Magic Shop, Weapon Shop, Henry quest references, battle/civic conflict references.
- Identity: fortified royal-market town where command presence and equipment commerce overlap.
- Concept roles: fortified market overall; command/Rorik exterior; equipment-shop exterior; King's/command interior; equipment-shop interior; fortified market gate/trade street.
- Detail: [`../towns/rossdam/brief.md`](../towns/rossdam/brief.md).

- Identity: confident clan-built forge-and-feast community centered on craft, protection, shared meals, and hospitality.
- Concept roles: longhall/forge-centered overall; armor/weapon workshop exterior; magic shop/pub/longhall exterior; forge/shop interior; pub/feast/magic interior; feast/workshop public space with gate/drainage/loading.

## Cambridge
- Coordinator-backed required anchors: docks/working waterfront, waterfront architecture, bridges/water crossings, water-oriented civic infrastructure, immediate water-centric read.
- Historical-source gap: no Cambridge-named map/interior was recovered from either pinned connected repository.
- Derived Project North Star anchors: Harbor Market Hall, Boatyard / Boatwright Shed, Canal Inn, Tidehouse / Cistern Court, Bridge Gate.
- Concept roles: canal/harbor overall; civic waterfront exterior; boatyard/inn exterior; market/tidehouse interior; boatyard/inn interior; bridge/quay/cistern public space.
- Detail: [`../towns/cambridge/brief.md`](../towns/cambridge/brief.md).

## Cross-town lock

Every concept must use a listed subject and show town-specific infrastructure in addition to its primary subject. Source-backed facts and derived Project North Star choices must remain labeled as such. The seven sets share one storybook visual language while staying distinguishable by silhouette/topology, materials/palette, economy/culture, terrain, and infrastructure.
