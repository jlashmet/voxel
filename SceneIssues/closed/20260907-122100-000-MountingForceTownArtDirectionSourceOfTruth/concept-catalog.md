# Mounting Force town concept catalog

The canonical roster was frozen before concept generation: **Kentridge, Hightown, Moordell, Rossdam, Orc Village / Orc-Town, Fairy Village, Cambridge**.

Each town directory contains a finished `concept-atlas.svg` plus six separately addressable role images. The atlas centralizes that town's palette/material/shape language; each numbered SVG renders exactly one semantic concept role for downstream worldbuilding use. This avoids duplicating embedded art while preserving one repository image artifact per required role.

## Visual acceptance audit

The seven packs share the Project North Star principles—stylized/readable construction, natural tactile material families, warm/cool lighting contrast, strong silhouettes, environmental integration, and no gameplay UI or character concepting—but they do **not** use recolor-only differentiation. Their topology/infrastructure signatures are distinct before color: Kentridge's well-centered organic lanes; Hightown's steep terraces/church; Moordell's formal plaza/avenues; Rossdam's gate/market axis; Orc Village's heavy longhall/forge court; Fairy Village's tree platforms/bridges; Cambridge's canals/quays/crossings.

Earlier schematic/blockout studies were rejected as below the issue's production-art bar and are not part of the source of truth. The committed atlases are the final authored storybook environment illustrations for this SceneIssue; their role files are the canonical downstream entry points.
