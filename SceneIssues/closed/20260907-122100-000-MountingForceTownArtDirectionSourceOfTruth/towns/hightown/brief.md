# Hightown concept brief

Source inventory is locked in `../../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md` before concept generation.

**Identity:** wind-bright terraced hill settlement centered on church, civic gathering and steep circulation. **Architecture:** pale stone, charcoal slate, tall narrow gables, buttressing, covered landings and arcades. **Palette:** chalk, charcoal, desaturated blue, plum, silvered timber, amber interiors. **Terrain/infrastructure:** stacked terraces, stairs, switchback cart road, retaining walls, stone gutters, gate/arch transitions and distribution forecourt. **Source-backed anchors:** church, two under-church levels, mayor house, armor/magic/weapon shops, pub, Timmy house/back room, cave and distribution event. **Six-image read:** church-dominant overall district; church exterior; terrace pub/shop exterior; church/under-church interior; pub/shop/house interior; stepped distribution forecourt.

The six role views consume `concept-atlas.svg`; environment reference only, with no character or UI design.
