# Cambridge concept brief

Source inventory is locked in `../../town-art-direction-source-of-truth.md` before concept generation.

No Cambridge-specific building/map file was recovered from either pinned historical Mounting Force repository. Cambridge is included because the SceneIssue/coordinator requirement is canonical for this assignment, and only that requirement's **water-town** identity is treated as source-backed: docks/working waterfront, waterfront architecture, bridges or crossings, water-oriented public infrastructure, and an immediate water-centric read. The venue names below are **derived Project North Star anchors**, not historical claims.

**Identity:** bright, industrious canal-and-dock town whose streets, trade and public life are inseparable from water. **Architecture:** pale limestone quays, cream/aqua plaster, weathered oak galleries, terracotta roofs, water-level doors, arcaded waterfront frontage, balconies and cranes. **Palette:** limestone cream, weathered oak, cobalt/aqua, oxidized copper, terracotta and sailcloth. **Infrastructure:** docks, slips, cranes, bollards, stairs to water, repeated arched bridges, culverts/storm drains, tide/flood markers, towpaths and a civic cistern/fountain court. **Derived anchors:** Harbor Market Hall, Boatyard/Boatwright Shed, Canal Inn, Tidehouse/Cistern Court and Bridge Gate. **Six-image read:** canal/harbor overall; Harbor Market Hall/Tidehouse exterior; boatyard/Canal Inn exterior; civic/market interior; boatyard/inn interior; bridge/quay/cistern public space.

The six role views consume `concept-atlas.svg`; environment reference only, with no character or UI design.
