# Experiment 002 — concept quality discriminator

## Hypothesis
A concept set can satisfy category/count coverage while still failing the issue if it reads as schematic/blockout art rather than implementation-guiding production art.

## Action
The first schematic/vector studies were reviewed against the repository's visual acceptance bar: intentional silhouettes, believable construction, material separation, terrain/infrastructure integration, readable composition, and sufficient authored detail for downstream implementation decisions.

## Result
The first studies were rejected and were not promoted into the source-of-truth pack. The replacement concept packs are finished multi-layered storybook illustrations with town-specific construction, topology, palette, landmark and infrastructure reads. Each town also exposes six semantic role images so category coverage can be audited without treating a single collage as six deliverables.

## Verdict
Primitive/count evidence is insufficient. The final town atlases + separately addressable role images are the accepted concept artifacts; rejected schematic studies are non-canonical.
