# R07 — Execution checklist

Budget: 45–60 minutes active work, excluding queued CI. Follow [plan.md](plan.md).

- [ ] Select a real authored arch and trace positive/carve primitive and material/style semantics into its far payload.
- [ ] Capture or inspect exact-player near/far views at the actual handoff; record distance/FOV/pixel resolution and geometry revisions.
- [ ] Record which opening, silhouette and material relationships survive, projected error in pixels, and visual classification. Counts/bounds alone cannot pass.
- [ ] Deliver the minimum far representation contract and evidence of whether a local proxy fix suffices. Defer simplification/CSG/impostor implementation choices.
- [ ] Record findings, artifact provenance and limitations; update the concise plan with material results and falsified hypotheses. A test failure may be the investigation result, never a successful validation claim.
- [ ] Review the issue-only diff and complete documentation checks. Close only if investigation acceptance is satisfied; otherwise retain the blocker in `open/`. Follow the canonical PR/merge workflow and verify the result on master.
