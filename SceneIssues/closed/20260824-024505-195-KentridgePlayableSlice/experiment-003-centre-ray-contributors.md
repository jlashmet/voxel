# Experiment 003 — centre-ray catalogue contributors

## Hypothesis

The huge wall is generated by a specific Kentridge structure whose instance bounds overlap the
saved camera, rather than a renderer-only floating mesh or an unidentified terrain surface.

## What was performed

Against source `36cec6893239e000c9aa875ebe9320a99927d0f4`, added a temporary EditMode diagnostic
that evaluates every Kentridge catalogue instance and samples its fill primitives along the exact
saved camera's centre ray. Ran
`VoxelEngine.Tests.EditMode.KentridgeGenerationTests.DiagnosticSavedFloatingStructureRayContributors`
through `tools/unity-run.sh`.

## Result

The test passed 1/1 and identified the near contributor as `kentridge-role-magicshop`, precedence
100, placement `(988,247,562)`. Its principal masonry box spans `(1014,254,572)` through
`(1085,355,639)` and contains the camera origin `(1039.305,263.500,605.096)` before the structure's
interior carve is applied. More distant ray hits are the armour and weapon shops. Evidence is
`verification-ray-contributors-results.xml` and `verification-ray-contributors-unity.log`.

## What was learned

The hypothesis is confirmed. The camera is inside the magic-shop footprint looking toward its west
wall; the apparent floating object is authored structure geometry. The remaining question is why
that named plot occupies the square/camera route and whether its orientation/plot spacing violates
the settlement plan.

## Next

Inspect the magic-shop semantic plot, market-square/street bounds, frontage direction, neighboring
plots, and structure form to find the violated placement invariant.
